## How To Register Participants
1. **Participant Name**  
   Enter the full name of the participant or group.

2. **Category Selection**  
   Choose from cultural categories:  
   - Morna Music  
   - Funana Dance  
   - Batuque Performance  
   - Cachupa Cooking  

3. **Contact Information**  
   Provide email or phone number for notifications.

4. **Add Participant**  
   Click to register. Traditional Morna performers should check "Traditional Style" and specify their instrument. Funana groups must include dancer count.

5. **Export to CSV**  
   Saves all registrations to a spreadsheet with:  
   - Names  
   - Categories  
   - Contact info  
   - Fees paid  

6. **Participants Bank**  
   Shows all registered entries. Click any entry to view details.
